Privacy Policy

Sunny Side Down · Last updated: 13 September 2026

Sunny Side Down tells you which seat in a vehicle will stay out of the sun on your journey. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data. It is short because the app does very little.

The short version

There are no accounts and no servers of our own. We do not store your data, we cannot see it, and nothing you do in the app is linked to your identity. Your location is sent to two mapping providers purely to work out your route, and everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Location (approximate and precise). With your permission, the app reads your device's location for two purposes: to set the starting point of a trip, and — only if you tap "Follow me along" — to track your progress along the route while the app is open.

Location access is optional. If you decline it, or your device cannot provide it, you can type a starting point instead and the app works normally.

The app requests foreground location only. It cannot and does not access your location when it is closed or in the background.

Who your data is sent to

To calculate a route and to search for places, the app sends coordinates and the place names you type to two third-party services:

  • Mapbox — receives your start and destination coordinates to calculate the driving route. See Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • Photon, operated by Komoot — receives the text you type when searching for a place, and your approximate location to rank nearby results first. See Komoot's privacy policy.

These requests are sent only when you actively search for a place or request a route. All are encrypted in transit over HTTPS. We do not receive a copy of this data ourselves.

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following in its local storage on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and we have no way to read it:

  • Your recent destinations
  • Any journeys you save under "Regulars"
  • Your driver-side preference (left- or right-hand drive)

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or personal details
  • No analytics or tracking software
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
  • No selling or sharing of data for advertising or marketing

Deleting your data

Because everything the app remembers is stored on your device, uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also revoke location access at any time in your device's system settings.

Children

Sunny Side Down is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
